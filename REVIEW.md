# Review list

499 entries. Everything below was either read from a photo with some doubt, written by the app
rather than printed in the book, or rests on a judgement. Have someone who knows Finnish well go
through it before relying on the app for an exam.

The book (*Oma suomi 2*, chapters 1–4) is monolingual: **every English gloss in the app was written
for it**. Grammar explanations, example sentences, hints and drills are also the app's own; the
book's rules are followed, its exercises are not copied.

Entries are grouped by chapter. Page numbers refer to the book.
